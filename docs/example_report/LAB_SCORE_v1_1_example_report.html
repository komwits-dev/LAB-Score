Overview

LAB-Score v1.1 integrates safety screening, gastrointestinal survival modules, probiotic-associated functions, and fermentation-related features into a continuous score and refined candidate tier.

Interpretation: LAB-Score is a decision-support framework for selecting candidate genomes for downstream experimental validation. It is not definitive proof of probiotic efficacy or safety.
